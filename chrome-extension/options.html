❓ Need Help?

How to get your CRM API Key:

  1. Log in to your Bitzsol CRM dashboard
  2. Go to Settings → API Keys
  3. Click "Generate New Key"
  4. Copy and paste the key above

How to use enrichment services:

Enrichment services are optional. If configured, they will provide additional contact information (emails, phone numbers) when you click the enrichment buttons in the extension popup.

Data Privacy:

All settings are stored locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere except to the services you configure.
